🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

ThreatLens ("we", "our", "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy across both the ThreatLens Android Mobile Application and the ThreatLens Browser Extension. This Privacy Policy details our strict zero-log principles, cryptographic verification protocols, and how data is processed.

1. Zero-Log Local Heuristics Principle

Whenever you scan a QR code, inspect a WiFi configuration, or hover over a link:

  • On-Device Heuristics: Pattern matching, homograph detection, and structural analyses are executed 100% locally on your device or in-browser memory.
  • No Tracking: Scanned content is never logged, sold, or shared for advertising purposes.

2. Browser Extension Privacy & Permissions

The ThreatLens Chrome & Edge Extension operates under minimal required permissions:

  • declarativeNetRequest: Used exclusively to block known tracking scripts and malicious ad networks before they execute. ThreatLens does not log or monitor your browsing history.
  • webNavigation: Used to intercept high-risk malicious redirect chains before page execution.
  • Local Storage: Scan history is saved within your local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) and remains private to your device unless you log into Cloud Sync.

3. AI Intelligence & Third-Party Threat Engines

To evaluate complex, zero-day threat destinations, ThreatLens consults trusted intelligence providers:

  • LLM7.io Fast Engine & Google Gemini: Evaluates destination heuristics to produce natural-language safety explanations and categorize domains. No personal account credentials or private identity data are ever sent to AI endpoints.
  • Threat Intelligence Feeds: VirusTotal, Google Safe Browsing, and URLhaus are queried to cross-reference known malware signatures.

4. Cloud Sync & Account Data (Optional)

If you choose to register and enable cross-device cloud synchronization:

  • Your user profile, scan history, and custom QR campaigns are securely stored in Google Firebase Firestore with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Passwords are hashed before transmission and are never stored in plaintext.

5. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

You have full ownership of your data and can permanently delete your account at any time:

  • In the Android App: Navigate to Settings → Profile Section → Delete Account.
  • In the Browser Extension: Navigate to Settings & Cloud → Sign Out / Clear Data.
  • Upon confirmation, all personal profiles, cloud-synced scan records, and campaign data are irrevocably wiped from Firebase Firestore.
