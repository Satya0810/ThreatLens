🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

ThreatLens ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and an encrypted password hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

Scan Data: QR codes and URLs you scan are analyzed locally on your device using on-device ML models. Scan history (URLs, timestamps, safety scores) is stored locally on your device.

Cloud Sync (Optional): If you enable cloud sync, your scan history and QR campaigns are stored in Google Firebase Firestore, linked to your user account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide QR code scanning and threat analysis
  • To maintain your scan history and favorites
  • To sync your data across devices (if enabled)
  • To generate and manage QR codes in QR Studio
  • To improve the accuracy of our threat detection algorithms

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase with industry-standard encryption. Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before storage. On-device ML analysis runs locally and does not send your scanned content to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

ThreatLens uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, cloud storage
  • Google ML Kit — On-device barcode scanning (no data sent to Google)
  • VirusTotal API — URL reputation checking (URLs may be sent for analysis)
  • Google Safe Browsing — URL safety verification

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. URLs scanned may be checked against third-party threat databases (VirusTotal, Google Safe Browsing) for safety analysis.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app
  • Delete Scan History: Use the "Clear History" button in the History screen
  • Delete Account: Use the "Delete" button in Settings → Profile to permanently delete your account and all associated data
  • Export: You can export your scan history as PDF from the app

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  • Open ThreatLens → Settings → Profile Section
  • Tap the "Delete" button
  • Enter your password to confirm
  • Your account, profile data, and all cloud-synced data will be permanently deleted

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing satyamkumar24052006@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

ThreatLens is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us
